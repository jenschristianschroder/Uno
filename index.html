THE HOUSE RULES

One card closer.

Match the top card’s color or symbol. First to empty their hand wins.

↷ Skip

The next player misses a turn.

⇄ Reverse

Switch direction. With two players, you go again.

+2 / +4

The next player draws and misses a turn. No stacking.

✦ Wild

Pick a color. Play +4 only if you have no card in the current color.

Draw one if you need to. You may play that drawn card or pass. An unplayable draw ends your turn.

Down to two cards? Tap “Call UNO”, then play. Forget to call before leaving one card and you draw two automatically.

A relaxed UNO-style variant: number-card starts, no +4 challenges, and single-round wins. Unofficial; not affiliated with Mattel.

Your table, your phones.

No accounts or game database. The host saves the round on their phone; guests save a seat token. A public PeerJS service and Google STUN help connect phones and see connection metadata. Game messages travel through encrypted WebRTC connections. Hosting and connection providers may keep access logs.

Keep the game open and your screen awake. If the host leaves, play pauses until they return. Some mobile or restricted networks will not connect without an additional TURN relay. Share the code only with your group.
